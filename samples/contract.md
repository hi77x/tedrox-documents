# Services Agreement

This agreement is entered into between **TEDROX Labs** ("the Provider") and the
client named in the order form ("the Client").

## 1. Scope

The Provider supplies local-first document software. No client data is
transmitted to the Provider or to any third party as part of normal operation.

## 2. Term

The agreement begins on the effective date and continues for twelve months
unless terminated earlier in writing by either party with thirty days notice.

## 3. Fees

Fees are invoiced quarterly. Amounts are due within thirty days of the invoice
date.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only
to perform this agreement.

## 5. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. The total
liability of either party is limited to the fees paid in the preceding twelve
months.

## 6. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction stated in the order
form.

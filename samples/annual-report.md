# Annual Report 2026

## Overview

TEDROX Documents processed **4.2 million** local operations this year. The engine
handled documents, spreadsheets and PDFs without sending a single byte to a
remote service.

## Highlights

- Every release ships with checksums and reproducible builds.
- The CLI and the desktop application share one engine.
- Format support is described by automated tests, not by marketing copy.

## Regional results

1. Europe: 1,840,000 operations.
2. Americas: 1,320,000 operations.
3. Asia-Pacific: 1,040,000 operations.

## Outlook

The next cycle focuses on deeper OOXML fidelity, a real paginated editor and
portable releases for Linux and Android.

*Prepared by the TEDROX Documents team.*
